SLATE·SPACE

Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

SLATE·SPACE is a personal workspace for tickets, time, transports and knowledge. This page explains which data it keeps, why, and how you stay in control of it.

What we store

  • Your account: email address, name and – if you sign in with Google – your Google profile picture.
  • Your work: the tickets, time entries, notes, tests, files, transports, objects and articles you create.
  • Settings: your preferences, such as the daily email time and time zone.

Your data is private to you. People only see a project when you share it with them, and only that project.

Google Calendar

If you connect Google Calendar, SLATE·SPACE uses Google's permission to read your events (to show your meetings in the Calendar page) and to create events you ask it to create (meetings you add in SLATE·SPACE). It does not change or delete existing events, does not read other Google data, and does not store your events – they are fetched when you open the calendar. The access is kept encrypted on our server and can be removed at any time in Account → Calendar or in your Google account settings.

SLATE·SPACE's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is never sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.

Where the data lives

Data is stored with Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage). Emails – sign-in codes and the optional daily summary – are sent through Resend. The site is served by Cloudflare, which also runs the invisible bot check on sign-in.

Cookies and tracking

There is no advertising and no tracking. The browser stores your sign-in session and display preferences (for example the theme); nothing else.

Your choices

  • Change or export your data at any time (Excel / CSV export in the app).
  • Switch off the daily email or disconnect Google Calendar in Account.
  • Delete your account in Account → Delete account; everything you stored is removed.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen of SLATE·SPACE.
